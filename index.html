Summary

14 years in QA has taught me that the job isn’t about catching every bug. It’s about giving teams the information they need to make confident release decisions. Most of that time I’ve been the person setting the testing strategy: figuring out where the real risk sits, what’s worth automating, and how to get engineering and product aligned on what building the right product means.
My background mixes hands-on automation work (Playwright, Selenium, Cypress, API) with team leadership and stakeholder communication. I’ve owned QA end-to-end across SaaS platforms, Salesforce ecosystems, and mobile apps, often as the main QA voice in the room.
I build AI-assisted QA workflows that analyze failed CI runs, flag flaky tests, and restore test data when it causes failures. That cuts down on manual triage so I can focus on failures that may point to product defects. I’m also exploring how QA’s focus is shifting from verification toward evaluation as AI and autonomous agents become part of the software we test.
